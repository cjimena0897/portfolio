I’m a UX UI designer with a background in Architecture, and I’ve turned my passion for understanding people into inclusive and accessible digital solutions.

Architecture brought me closer to users, always focusing on enhancing their experience by addressing their needs while considering accessibility and sustainability. This is how sketches evolved into wireframes and models transformed into prototypes, leading me to become a digital solutions architect today.

Today I design user-centered digital experiences — from user interviews and research to interfaces and working prototypes — looking for solutions that make a positive impact.
